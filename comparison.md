# PokéBonk vs Megabonk

[Megabonk](https://megabonk.org/) started the 3D survivor wave. [PokéBonk Free](https://pokebonkfree.online/) takes the format into Kanto: 151 Pokémon, starter evolutions, shinies, gym-structured Classic mode.

## Shared
Waves, auto-attacks, mid-run level-up choices, run-based progression.

## PokéBonk adds
The Kanto roster with per-line evolution timing; Classic gym structure; shiny variants as a long-term chase.

## Playing both
Complementary. More on [the guide home](/pokebonk-guide/). English free version: [pokebonkfree.online](https://pokebonkfree.online/)
