# PokéBonk Free — Quick Start Guide

**[PokéBonk Free](https://pokebonkfree.online/)** is a free 3D Pokémon survivor roguelite that runs entirely in your browser: pick a Kanto starter, fight through waves of wild Pokémon, and survive as long as possible while your team levels up and evolves.

**Play now:** [pokebonkfree.online](https://pokebonkfree.online/) — no download, no install, mouse only.

## Picking your starter

| Starter | Style | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Bulbasaur | Control | Easiest early game, status moves handle big waves |
| Charmander | Damage | Glass cannon, great once you learn kiting |
| Squirtle | Tank | Forgiving pick if you keep getting surrounded |

## Survival tips

1. **Keep moving.** Standing still is how runs end — circle hordes instead of pushing through.
2. **Level one Pokémon at a time.** A single evolved carry beats three underleveled picks.
3. **Bank heal pickups before the 10 minute mark.** The mid-game difficulty spike is real.
4. **Use choke points.** One tanky Pokémon can hold a whole wave in a corridor.

## Modes

- **Classic** — the full Kanto run with a gym-by-gym difficulty curve.
- **Survival** — endless waves, pure score chasing.
