# Survival builds — three archetypes

Three build archetypes for Survival mode in [PokéBonk Free](https://pokebonkfree.online/):

## Corridor Tank (Squirtle line)
Defensive slots, hold the widest choke. First-time Survival players commonly clear wave 15+.

## Score Chaser (Charmander line)
Offense stacking, open-field kiting. Leaderboard ceiling, thin margins.

## Control Lock (Bulbasaur line)
Status upgrades from wave 6. Sleep-lock mixed waves; lowest mechanical demand for consistent 20 minute runs.
