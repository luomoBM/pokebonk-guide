# Survival tips — extended

Ten quick habits for longer Survival runs in [PokéBonk Free](https://pokebonkfree.online/):

1. Circle, don't push. Hordes chase your last position.
2. One carry until evolution, always.
3. Heals before the 10 minute spike — not after.
4. Corridors cut fifteen chasers down to two.
5. Status moves stop waves entirely; damage only shrinks them.
6. Upgrade menus: pause first, the leaderboard timer doesn't wait.
7. Edge-standing is the early-game farm; mid-game rotates between chokes.
8. Late waves change the objective: surviving *is* the scoring.
9. Shiny spawns are per-roll — Survival farms them fastest.
10. If you wipe, retry a level higher rather than re-routing.
