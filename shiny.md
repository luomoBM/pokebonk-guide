# Shiny hunting — efficient farming notes

Shiny spawns in [PokéBonk Free](https://pokebonkfree.online/) roll per-spawn. Nothing in your build changes the rate — the efficient farm is simply the mode with the most spawns per minute.

**Survival beats Classic** for spawn throughput. Fast, comfortable clears at wave 12-18 produce more spawn events per hour than pushing for deep runs.

**Keep shinies in the team:** they keep their palette through evolution — an evolved shiny is both trophy and carry.

**Aggregate over runs:** hundreds of spawns per run means expected wait is measured in runs, not sessions. Farm XP routes normally and let rolls accumulate.
