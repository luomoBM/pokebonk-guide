## Movement
The horde chases your cursor's last position. Wide arcs keep spawns stacked behind your auto-attack coverage; sharp reversals let flankers clip you.

## Menus
Classic pauses for upgrade picks — read freely. Survival's leaderboard timer keeps running between picks, so pre-plan priority order before the wave starts.

## Cursor lead
Placing the cursor where the wave will be (not where it is) keeps coverage tight without constant kiting effort.

## Accessibility
One-hand play, no keyboard handoffs, no combo memory. Runs stay sustainable for long sessions.
