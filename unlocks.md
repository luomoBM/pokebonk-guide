# League unlocks — how the 79 roster opens up

The League roster in [PokéBonk Free](https://pokebonkfree.online/) — 79 league-legal Pokémon — unlocks through Classic gym progression. Clearing each gym expands the next area's spawn pool, and Survival rolls from whatever your progression has unlocked (permanent).

## Efficient order
Clear gyms in sequence. Each expansion includes the coverage types the next gym needs — the intended order is also the smoothest.

## Shiny note
Any unlocked spawn can roll shiny, including early-area ones. Survival farming covers the aggregation.
